THE CHALLENGE

COAST OVER is the first-ever continuous relay swim across the central Adriatic Sea, from San Benedetto del Tronto (Italy) to Zadar (Croatia).

A team of 6 swimmers + 1 reserve will rotate 24/7, accompanied by support and rescue vessels, to cover approximately 170 km (92 nautical miles) of open water.

The 2027 edition is the pilot project for what is envisioned as an annual international sporting event.

TEAM & FORMAT

// Swim Team

  • 6 swimmers rotating in fixed order
  • 1 reserve swimmer (medical substitution)
  • 1-hour shifts, 5 hours rest between shifts
  • Continuous 24/7 swimming
  • Changeover in the water alongside escort boat

// Support Fleet

  • Mother ship (15-20m) — base of operations
  • Escort RIB (6-8m) — accompanies active swimmer
  • Rescue RIB (5-6m) — dedicated safety boat
  • Media boat — video, drone, documentation
  • Sports doctor on board 24/7
  • Official observer (FIN certified)
05 / 10

SAFETY PROTOCOL

  • Full-time sports physician with AED defibrillator
  • 2-3 maritime rescue operators on dedicated vessel
  • Direct coordination with Italian Coast Guard and Croatian MRCC Rijeka
  • MEDEVAC plan with position updates every 15 minutes
  • Satellite phone + VHF radio (primary & backup comms)
  • Shark Shield electromagnetic deterrent devices
  • GPS trackers, signal buoys, night lighting, thermal blankets
  • Comprehensive marine sports insurance (liability + injury)
  • 5+ day weather window protocol before departure
